**Goal:** Build a lightning-fast, GPU-accelerated text editor.

**Stack:** Rust (core) and C (bindings). No Electron. No JavaScript.

**Core:** Based on the modernized `xi-editor` core.

**Concept:** "Lumen" stands for speed and clarity. The editor should open instantly and get out of the developer's way.

### Status

[Core](/projects/xi-editor). is stabilized and builds on recent Rust (forked from the abandoned xi-editor). Frontend development is next.
